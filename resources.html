Everyday EQ

In a world where AI accelerates tasks and information moves faster than attention, Emotional Intelligence has become the ultimate human edge. EVERYDAY EQ is a practical, science-backed guide that helps you strengthen that edge through simple, repeatable habits that fit perfectly into your real life—no hours of training, no complex theories, and no overwhelm.

Drawing on more than a decade of teaching high-performance programs, this book breaks EQ into three clear parts: the foundations that steady your system, the core skills that shape relationships, and the daily practices that turn awareness into action. Through 70+ Micropractices and 14 Anchor Practices, you will learn how to notice inner signals, regulate reactions, communicate clearly, navigate conflict, and read the room with precision.
