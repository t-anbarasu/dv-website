Our Proprietary Methodology

The CLEAR Method for Practical AI

Before you type a single prompt, we guide you through our proven 5-step framework to ensure AI delivers genuine ROI.

C

Clarify

Define the exact business outcome, target customer value, and definition of done for the chosen workflow.

L

Locate

Identify repetitive friction points, manual data re-entry, and communication bottlenecks draining team hours.

E

Eliminate

Strip out redundant approvals, obsolete form fields, and useless steps before introducing any technology.

A

Automate & Augment

Deploy practical AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, automation connectors) with customized prompt templates.

R

Review & Refine

Establish quality check guardrails, track time saved, and fine-tune output accuracy week over week.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a technical or coding background to attend? ▾
Not at all. SimplifAI is specifically designed for non-technical business owners and managers. If you can use WhatsApp and send an email, you will be able to follow every concept and build your own AI tools during the lab.
What should I bring to the workshop? ▾
Bring your own laptop or tablet (a smartphone is also acceptable for lighter tasks) with an active internet connection. Most importantly, bring 1 or 2 real processes in your business that currently cause you headaches or take too much manual time.
Can the workshop be conducted entirely in Tamil? ▾
Yes. We frequently conduct cohorts fully in Tamil (or blended bilingual Tamil & English) so that language is never an obstacle to mastering cutting-edge tools.
How is this different from free tutorials or YouTube videos? ▾
Online videos teach you generic prompt tricks in a vacuum. SimplifAI focuses first on process engineering — eliminating wasteful steps so you don't automate chaos. Furthermore, you receive live hands-on troubleshooting and personalized guidance on your specific business workflow.
How does the 30–90 day SimplifAI Consulting engagement work? ▾
Our consulting engagement is an end-to-end operational partnership. We audit your workflows, build custom AI solutions and automation pipelines, train your staff to operate them reliably, and monitor performance weekly until the new productivity baseline is permanently achieved.
