The Mid-Size Growth Trap

Why Internal Training Breaks Down in 50–500 Person Companies

When an organization expands beyond 50 employees, informal mentorship stops working. But building a full-time corporate training team comes with severe pitfalls.

💸

High Payroll Overhead

Hiring an L&D head, instructional designer, and coordinators easily adds ₹25–40L+ annually to fixed payroll before a single workshop begins.

🎲

The Random Seminar Trap

Bringing in a disconnected motivational speaker once a year produces a temporary buzz on Monday and zero behavioral change by Wednesday.

📉

Zero Business Alignment

Training is rarely tied to revenue milestones, product rollouts, or operational quality metrics. Management cannot measure the return on investment.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does an outsourced virtual L&D service work day-to-day? ▾
You are assigned a dedicated Drishta Vidya L&D Director who coordinates with your HR lead and business heads. We manage everything: conducting training needs analysis, designing the annual calendar, building learning materials, scheduling sessions, facilitating live workshops, and sending quarterly executive reports.
What size organization is LEAD best suited for? ▾
LEAD is ideal for organizations with 50 to 500 employees. At this size, hiring a full in-house corporate university is economically inefficient, yet informal training is no longer adequate to maintain quality and culture.
Can LEAD cover specialized technical or industry training? ▾
Yes. While Drishta Vidya's core faculty directly facilitates leadership, emotional intelligence, management skills, and AI adoption, our L&D management architecture can curate, onboard, and evaluate specialized external technical subject matter experts for your unique domain.
What is the minimum engagement commitment? ▾
We recommend a minimum initial commitment of 6 months. Behavioral transformation and competency development require structured quarterly cycles to diagnose, deliver, reinforce, and measure meaningful business impact.
How do we get started? ▾
Simply fill out our contact form or book a discovery call. We will review your current organizational structure, discuss your growth bottlenecks, and propose an initial diagnostic assessment for your team.
