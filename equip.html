Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About EQuip

How does the 60-day EQ OS follow-up work? ▾
Immediately following the 2-day workshop, each participant receives secure login credentials to EQ OS (at eqos.drishtavidya.com). Over the next 60 days, participants receive daily micro-prompts taking 2–3 minutes, personalized AI scenario coaching, and progress reflections to cement emotional regulation into permanent automatic habits.
Is participant data inside EQ OS private and secure? ▾
Yes, strictly. All reflections, journal entries, and conversations inside EQ OS are 100% private and encrypted. We maintain a zero-surveillance policy: employers and HR leaders receive aggregate participation and engagement metrics only, and cannot view personal reflections or emotional scores.
Can EQuip be delivered exclusively for our company on-site? ▾
Yes. We regularly conduct in-house bespoke editions of EQuip for corporate teams across India. We tailor case studies and practice scenarios to your company's actual operating dynamics and culture.
What is the ideal group size for an EQuip cohort? ▾
To ensure deep personal engagement, interactive simulations, and individual attention, cohorts are typically capped at 15 to 25 participants. For larger organizations, we run multiple concurrent or staggered cohorts.
Do participants receive a verified certificate? ▾
Yes. Participants who complete the 2-day live workshop and achieve the milestone participation in the 60-day EQ OS follow-up receive a verified Certificate of Completion in Emotional Intelligence from Drishta Vidya LLP.
