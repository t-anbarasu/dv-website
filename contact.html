FAQ

Common Questions

How long are the workshops?
Our EQuip and SimplifAI workshops run for 1 to 2 days, with EQuip followed by a structured 60-day follow-up journey in EQ OS. Our LEAD virtual L&D service is an ongoing monthly engagement tailored to your organizational calendar.
Can workshops be conducted online?
Yes. Our programs can be delivered in-person, virtually, or in a blended format depending on your team's needs and location.
Do you offer customized programs?
Absolutely. We work with organizations to design bespoke training experiences that address your specific challenges, culture, and growth goals. Contact us to start that conversation.
What is the ideal group size?
Our workshops work best with 20–30 participants per cohort, ensuring deep engagement and personalized facilitation. We can accommodate larger groups through multiple cohorts or customized enterprise delivery.
