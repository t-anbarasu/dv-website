What is EQ Sundays

A free weekly community session where you learn the practical side of Emotional Intelligence — not theory you forget by Tuesday, but real tools you can use in your next conversation, meeting, or moment of conflict. Hosted by Anbarasu Thangavelu, author of Everyday EQ, with occasional guest experts.

Sessions you can expect
💼
At Work
Your boss publicly criticised your work in a meeting
How do you respond without shutting down or blowing up? We'll practise the 10-second reset.
Boss · Criticism · Self-Regulation
A colleague takes credit for your idea — again
Why this triggers you so deeply, and a framework to address it without burning the relationship.
Colleague · Resentment · Boundaries
You need to give tough feedback to someone you like
Most people avoid it. Some do it badly. We'll learn to do it with empathy and clarity.
Team · Feedback · Difficult Conversations
The new change initiative is met with silence and resistance
It's not about the change — it's about the emotion behind the resistance. How to lead through it.
Leadership · Change · Empathy
🏠
At Home
Your teenager won't talk to you anymore
Why pushing harder makes it worse, and how emotional bids can rebuild the bridge — one small moment at a time.
Children · Connection · Emotional Bids
The same argument with your spouse — for the 50th time
Recurring fights aren't about the topic. They're about unmet emotional needs. We'll unpack what's really going on.
Spouse · Conflict Patterns · Needs
Your parents' advice feels like criticism
Separating intention from impact — and responding to what they meant, not what you heard.
Family · Triggers · Perspective
You lost your temper at your child and now feel guilty
Guilt is data, not a verdict. How to repair, reconnect, and build a calmer default response.
Children · Guilt · Repair
🪞
With Yourself
You say yes to everything and burn out every quarter
The EQ skill nobody talks about: setting boundaries without guilt. We'll practise saying no with grace.
Boundaries · People-Pleasing · Self-Awareness
Comparison is stealing your peace — at work and on social media
Understanding the envy-to-inspiration spectrum and rewiring your emotional response to others' success.
Envy · Self-Worth · Mindset
