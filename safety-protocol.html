1. Clear Boundaries of the Service

EQ OS and its AI companion, Drishti, are designed strictly as an educational self-help tool for building communication and emotional intelligence skills.

  • Not Therapy: EQ OS does not provide psychotherapy, medical treatment, psychological diagnosis, or clinical care.
  • AI Transparency: Drishti always identifies itself as an AI system and never claims human sentience or professional accreditation.
  • 18+ Only: The application is restricted to adults aged 18 and older.
  • No Medical, Legal, or Financial Advice: Drishti will actively decline requests for medical, diagnostic, legal, or financial counseling.

2. The 6-Step Safety & Escalation Protocol

Designed and overseen by our coaching lead, Dr. Gabriel, EQ OS enforces hard procedural safety gates:

Step 1: Multi-Layer Detection

Text inputs are analyzed against an exhaustive list of self-harm, crisis, and domestic abuse keywords in English, Tamil, and code-mixed formats on our secure server before reaching any conversational AI. In addition, checking in at high intensity (9–10) or on severe sadness/shame immediately triggers safety verification.

Step 2: Immediate Cessation of AI Coaching

If risk is detected, the system immediately ceases all automated generative responses. EQ OS never attempts to "coach" a user out of an acute psychological emergency.

Step 3: Direct Helpline Routing

The user is presented with the Crisis Route card featuring verified 24/7 helplines, one-tap calling buttons, and international referral links.

Step 4: Offer of Human Follow-Up

The user can optionally tap "Ask a coach to follow up". This transmits a minimal alert (name and chosen contact info) to the on-call Drishta Vidya coach. The personal text or journal entries that triggered the route are never forwarded.

Step 5: Privacy-Preserving Logging

The incident is recorded strictly as a numerical safety event (trigger type and whether follow-up was requested). No user free-text is stored beyond 30 days.

Step 6: Weekly Clinical Review

Every crisis event is reviewed weekly by our safety team to continually audit accuracy and refine support protocols.

3. Domestic Abuse & Toxic Relationships

In conflict situations, relationship tools check for indicators of intimidation, physical harm, or domestic abuse. The app does not advise accommodation or compromise in abusive environments; instead, it provides immediate access to protective legal and social helplines.
